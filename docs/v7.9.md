v7.9 continues the pinned Orange Cyberdefense 2025.03 source-depth queue from v7.8 by decomposing the two remaining `low_access.md` parents that were still partial at the frozen v6.2 boundary: Windows local exploit paths and WebDAV coercion.

The release models three atomic source units: SMBGhost/CVE-2020-0796 validation, HiveNightmare/SeriousSAM/CVE-2021-36934 validation, and the WebDAV/searchConnector-ms coercion chain. It adds focused human-run cards instead of treating broad source labels as delivery. The WebDAV card keeps DNS preparation, lure creation/opening, coercion trigger, inbound authentication, relay success, access, execution, privilege, and cleanup separate.

v7.9 deliberately does not mark the whole `low_access.md` file atomized. The other already-implemented low-access parents still need subordinate source inventory before the file-level denominator can advance. Canonical accounting advances only `low_access.local-exploit` and `low_access.webdav`, bringing the current canonical projection to 124/127 implemented and 3 partial while preserving zero gaps and zero stale mappings. The frozen source-depth denominator advances from 29/34 to 31/34, and the currently inventoried atomic ledger grows from 187/187 to 190/190 fidelity-complete units.

Obol remains static, offline-capable, browser-local, and human-run only. Commands are guidance for authorized environments and are never executed automatically by Obol.
