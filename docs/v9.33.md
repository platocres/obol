## Credential and authentication boundaries

- Passwords, reusable NT hashes, NetNTLM challenge-response captures, Kerberos tickets, certificates or keys, and session material are different material classes with different protocol compatibility.
- NetNTLMv1/v2 challenge-response captures are cracking inputs, not reusable pass-the-hash credentials.
- A recovered plaintext password or decrypted secret remains candidate material until an independent authentication result proves access to the specific target and service tested.
- Kerberos TGTs, service tickets, and later service access remain distinct protocol stages and Evidence states.
- SAM, LSASS, cached credentials, and similar stores are sources of authentication material, not access or privilege facts.
- Password guessing and spraying should account for lockout policy and observed defensive signals; failed authentication is scoped to the tested account, service, and moment.
- Unlocking an encrypted vault, archive, document, or private key produces a new secret or artifact, not proof of downstream target authentication.
- HTTP Basic authentication requires protected transport because its credential encoding is not encryption; successful authentication proves only the tested realm or resource.
