## Evidence boundaries

A readable AppLocker policy or writable directory is only context. It is not proof of application-control bypass. A scanner or PrivescCheck finding is a lead until the specific condition is manually verified. A `findstr` match is a credential candidate, not reusable access. KrbRelayUp relay/control, ticket material, process launch, and SYSTEM verification remain distinct states. SeImpersonatePrivilege itself does not prove SYSTEM; the PrintSpoofer alternative requires explicit post-transition identity output.

Superseded source variants remain visible in the atomic ledger so source fidelity is not achieved by omission.
