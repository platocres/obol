## Product behavior

The new fuzzer analyzer recognizes evidence such as Burp Intruder or ZAP Fuzzer workflow markers, payload-position setup, payload-processing or encoding configuration, response status/length/word-count deltas, candidate hits, and scope/rate constraints.

It intentionally treats fuzzer output as triage. A response delta can move the operator to manual replay and response-body review, but it does not become impact by itself.
