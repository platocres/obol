## Evidence boundaries

SCCM discovery is mapping evidence. DNS, SPN, computer, client-push, policy-registration, or relay-listener preparation does not establish inbound authentication. A coercion request does not prove a relay. A successful relay does not automatically prove authenticated service access, execution, administrator/SYSTEM context, or privilege.

Policy and database workflows also preserve material boundaries: machine-account hashes, SQL authentication, encrypted database values, decrypted credential material, validated credentials, access, execution, privilege, and cleanup are independent states.

Cleanup is explicit for temporary DNS/SPN/client/device state and SCCM resource artifacts. Cleanup actions require an exact reviewed object or GUID and produce their own Evidence rather than being assumed from exploit completion.
