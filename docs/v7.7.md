## Evidence boundaries

v7.7 keeps the following proof states independent:

- network/service discovery;
- listener state;
- DHCPv6 or ARP poison state;
- inbound authentication;
- captured hash or credential material;
- coercion trigger success;
- relay success;
- credential validation;
- authenticated access;
- remote execution;
- administrator or SYSTEM context;
- domain privilege;
- cleanup/restoration.

A successful mitm6 startup or DHCPv6 response never becomes a credential or relay fact. ARP spoofing state never becomes captured material. Pcredz output becomes credential/hash material only when explicit fields are present. A PetitPotam trigger never becomes inbound authentication or relay success without separate Evidence.
