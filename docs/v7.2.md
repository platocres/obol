## Permanent proof boundaries

ACL or ACE discovery is routing context, not effective control. Object-control rights are not a successful mutation. A successful mutation is not credential material, ticket material, authenticated access, execution, administrator access, privilege, or domain control. Credential retrieval and certificate/ticket acquisition remain artifacts until independently used and evidenced. Temporary ownership, DACL, SPN, Key Credential, RBCD, group-membership, password, OU/GPO, or related changes require explicit cleanup/restoration evidence where restoration is practical and part of the authorized test.
