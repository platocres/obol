## Product additions

### `linux-privesc-boundary-sweep`

A command-bearing Linux card for privilege-boundary triage. The card covers service-footprint checks, user-trail review, bounded `tcpdump`, `sudo -l`, SUID discovery, capabilities, scheduled execution checks, systemd/cron review, and kernel/distro context before exploit research. It teaches users to prove the precondition before taking state-changing or risky action and keeps credential-candidate discovery inside the same Linux privilege-escalation decision path.

### Evidence analyzers

- `linux-footprint-evidence-analyzer`
- `linux-privesc-boundary-analyzer`

The analyzers tag pasted terminal output as process secret candidates, user-trail candidates, tcpdump observations, sudo rules, SUID leads, capability leads, scheduled-execution leads, or kernel-precondition evidence. Summaries redact flags, IPs, and credential-like strings. Footprint findings report that they fold into `linux-privesc-boundary-sweep` rather than creating a duplicate card.
