## Product additions

### `linux-service-footprint-secret-review`

A command-bearing Linux card for service-footprint and user-trail evidence. The card covers bounded `ps`, `watch`, `env`, `grep`, `find`, `sudo -l`, and scoped `tcpdump` checks so process visibility, user-trail material, packet observations, candidate secrets, and validated credentials do not collapse into one sloppy finding.

### `linux-privesc-boundary-sweep`

A command-bearing Linux card for privilege-boundary triage. The card covers `sudo -l`, SUID discovery, capabilities, scheduled execution checks, systemd/cron review, and kernel/distro context before exploit research. It teaches users to prove the precondition before taking state-changing or risky action.

### Evidence analyzers

- `linux-footprint-evidence-analyzer`
- `linux-privesc-boundary-analyzer`

The analyzers tag pasted terminal output as process secret candidates, user-trail candidates, tcpdump observations, sudo rules, SUID leads, capability leads, scheduled-execution leads, or kernel-precondition evidence. Summaries redact flags, IPs, and credential-like strings.
